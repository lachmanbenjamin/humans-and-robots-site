About Humans and Robots, LLC

Humans and Robots, LLC is a social media consultancy founded by Benjamin Lachman in Metro Detroit, Michigan. The firm runs social media as a business channel for mission-driven businesses and organizations: building qualified audiences, turning comments and DMs into real conversations, handling customer complaints in public, and reporting every month on what social moved. Behind the feed, the firm sets up the CRM, lead capture, follow-up, and review systems that turn social activity into business results. It pairs human strategy and community management experience with AI-assisted execution.
