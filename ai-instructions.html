About Humans and Robots, LLC

Humans and Robots, LLC is an AI-powered consulting firm founded by Benjamin Lachman in Metro Detroit, Michigan. The firm helps small and mid-sized businesses adopt AI to run leaner, faster marketing and operations. Rather than selling generic AI hype, Humans and Robots pairs human strategy with AI execution to deliver concrete outcomes: content calendars built in under two hours, branded client-ready proposals generated in minutes, legacy paper records migrated into modern CRMs, and roughly 60% cost savings versus traditional agency or in-house staffing. The firm works as a continuous partner that identifies high-impact AI opportunities and implements them without disrupting how a business already runs.
