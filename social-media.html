Why most social doesn't move the business.

  • The best stories never get told.The people, the work, and the impact behind your mission sit on someone's phone. By the time anyone posts, the moment's gone.
  • There's no plan.Random posts when someone remembers. Nothing tied to your busy seasons or what you're trying to sell or fill.
  • Comments and DMs go unanswered.The conversations that turn into customers never happen, and complaints sit there in public.
  • You're measuring the wrong things.Likes and reach go up and down. Nobody can say whether social brought in a customer, kept one, or saved a bad week.
  • It feels like an ad, not a community.Stock photos and "buy now" captions. The people you're trying to reach scroll past.

Honest answers

What you're probably wondering.

"Do you just post AI-generated content?"
No. We use modern tools to move faster, but content is built from your real work, your people, and your voice. AI slop is easy to spot, and it doesn't build the kind of trust that turns into customers.
"How do you tie this to revenue?"
The Social Audit sets a baseline on six numbers. Each month we report on movement, including the leads, bookings, and sales we can trace to social: DMs that became customers, posts that drove a call, campaigns tied to a busy season.
"How much of my time does this take?"
Around an hour a month — usually a short call to look at the calendar, share what's coming up, and answer specific customer questions for the FAQ-style posts.
"What does it cost?"
Every engagement starts with the Social Audit: $1,250, fixed. Ongoing managed social typically starts around $3K/month after that. Scope adjusts based on platforms, paid spend, and how much production is involved. No long contracts; pause, scale, or stop monthly.
