The problem

Public data is everywhere. Operational data is rare.

Every week, governments and agencies publish hundreds of thousands of rows of high-value signal: who got a permit, who filed an 8-K, where storms are forecast, which contractors lost their license. The data is technically free. The cost is the week of engineering time you'd spend pulling it, cleaning it, deduplicating it, and fitting it to a workflow your team will actually use.

We do that work — once, well, on a schedule — and hand you the operational layer: flat files, dashboards, or a private data lake your tools can query. No demos. No "platform." Just the signal, on Tuesday morning.

Data sources & compliance

Built on public records. Operated like grown-ups.

Every record we deliver originates from a public, lawfully accessible source — municipal permit portals, state licensing boards, SEC EDGAR, NOAA / NWS, county assessor and parcel portals, FCC, FAA, USPTO, OpenStreetMap, and the like. We do not bypass authentication, paywalls, or terms of service.

We retain provenance metadata on every row — source URL, fetched-at timestamp, and normalization rule applied — so your compliance team can audit any record back to its origin. PII is handled on a per-jurisdiction basis; fields are redacted or hashed where local law requires it. We do not resell raw datasets and we do not train third-party models on client-defined schemas.

Coverage and refresh cadences depend on the publishing schedules of source agencies and may vary. We document known gaps in each weekly brief.

FAQ

Questions we get on every kickoff call.

Is this a SaaS product?

No. We deliver flat files and briefs to your inbox, Drive, Sheets, or warehouse. There's nothing to log into and no integration required. If you want it piped into Salesforce, HubSpot, Snowflake, or BigQuery, we'll set that up on the custom build.

What's actually in the $297 pilot?

Two consecutive weekly Permit Signal briefs with 25 records per brief, one trade or territory filter, public City source links, reasons each record made the list, and CSV delivery. No homeowner contacts and no guaranteed-lead claims.

How is this different from buying a list?

Lists are static; signal is recurring. We don't sell you a dump of last year's permits — we deliver this week's, with provenance, every week, in a schema that stays stable so your team can build a workflow around it.

Can you cover my city / county?

Probably. We cover 250+ municipalities today and add ~10 per week. If yours isn't in the live lake, we'll add it as part of pilot onboarding when feasible, or quote a one-time backfill.

What about SEC, NOAA, and property assessment reports?

The SEC Filing Signal lake is in active Q1 build; the NOAA Weather-Risk lake is in Q2 build. Property Assessment Signal is in the research queue and would be informational only: no tax-savings promises, no appeal guarantees, and no legal advice. Join a waitlist and we'll bring you in as a design partner when the offer is scoped.

Do you offer custom schemas or warehouse delivery?

Yes — that's the Custom Data-Lake Build offer. We'll define a schema contract, deliver into your Snowflake / BigQuery / Postgres, and set an SLA. Talk to us to scope.

What if the signal doesn't land for my team?

Tell us during the pilot window. We'll use the feedback to tighten the filter, explain source gaps, or recommend that you do not subscribe. Refunds are not offered by default, but billing errors, duplicate charges, disputes, and delivery failures are escalated for review.
