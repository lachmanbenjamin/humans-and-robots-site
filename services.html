The Social Audit

A fixed-fee, two-week look at what social is actually doing for your business, scored against the same six numbers we report on every month. $1,250, fixed.

Outcome we promise

A baseline scorecard, a clear picture of where social is leaking leads, trust, and time, and a ranked 90-day plan you can run with or without us.

What's included

  • Account and content review. The last 90 days across your active platforms: what you posted, what landed, and what nobody saw.
  • Audience quality check. Who actually follows and engages, and how many of them look like customers, partners, or advocates.
  • Conversation and response review. How fast comments and DMs get answered, which ones never do, and where inquiries go after that.
  • Reputation scan. Reviews, mentions, and public complaints, and how they were handled.
  • Peer benchmark. What two or three comparable organizations are doing that you aren't.
  • Baseline scorecard and 90-day plan. Every fix ranked by impact and effort, walked through together on a 60-minute call.

Qualified audience Conversations to leads Advocacy & reviews Service recovery

If the audit shows social isn't the right lever for you right now, we'll say so plainly and tell you what is.

Service 02

Conversations & customer care

The comments and DMs are where social turns into business, or where it quietly falls apart. We make sure every real question gets a fast, human answer, and every upset customer gets handled before it becomes a screenshot.

Outcome we promise

Same-day replies on comments and DMs, complaints turned around in public, and real inquiries routed straight into your follow-up.

What's included

  • Comment and DM management. Answered in your voice, on a defined response time.
  • DM automation. Smart first replies, FAQs, and keyword triggers that qualify an inquiry and hand it to a person, not a dead end.
  • Lead routing. Inquiries from social land in your CRM or inbox with the right details attached.
  • Service recovery playbook. How to respond when someone is unhappy in public: who answers, how fast, and what to say.
  • Escalation rules. What gets handled by us, what goes to you, and what gets flagged right away.
